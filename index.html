About

Bridging the classroom, the lab, and campus technology

Kinnari Mishra is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Engineering Department at PIET‑DS, Parul University, and currently serves as Manager of the university's ICT Cell. Her work sits at the intersection of teaching, applied research, and academic technology — from classifier optimisation and text mining to training faculty on AI‑assisted research tools.

She is pursuing a Ph.D. at Parul University (since 2024) and holds an M.C.A. from North Gujarat University, completed with First Class with Distinction. Her research record spans nine peer‑reviewed publications and three authored books on programming and data mining, alongside sustained mentorship of undergraduate project teams.

Beyond the classroom, she leads faculty training in emerging tools — generative AI for research, LaTeX, Mentimeter, and the university's LMS — and has held teaching and lecturing roles across four institutions prior to joining Parul University.
